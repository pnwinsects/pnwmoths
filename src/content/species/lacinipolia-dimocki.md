## Habitat

*Lacinipolia dimocki* occurs in dry open forest habitat, often in association with oaks.

## Distribution

### Pacific Northwest

This species occurs in western Oregon and near the Columbia Gorge in south-central Washington. The distribution of this moth is probably underrepresented on this site due to confusion with Lacinipolias, particularly *Lacinipolia sareta*.

### Global

The range of this West Coast moth extends to southern California.

## Economic Importance

None.
