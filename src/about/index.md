# About PNW Moths

Pacific Northwest Moths is an open-source resource for identifying and learning
about the moths of Washington, Oregon, Idaho, Montana, and British Columbia. The
site brings together species fact sheets, photographic plates, distribution
records, and an identification key, built from the collections and expertise of
many institutions and individuals across the region.
