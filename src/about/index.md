# About PNW Moths

Pacific Northwest Moths is an open-source resource for identifying and learning
about the moths of Washington, Oregon, Idaho, Montana, and British Columbia. The
site brings together species fact sheets, photographic plates, distribution
records, and an identification key, built from the collections and expertise of
many institutions and individuals across the region.

## In this section

- [About the Data](/about/data/) — where the occurrence records come from, how they are cleaned, and known limitations.
- [About the Images](/about/images/) — image licensing, photography methods, and a guide to making photographic plates.
- [References](/about/references/) — bibliography of works informing the taxonomy, identification key, and species accounts.
- [Site Credits](/about/credits/) — the people and institutions behind the site.
- [Contact](/contact/) — questions, comments, or data to share.
